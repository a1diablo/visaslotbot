Why VFS London slots are so hard to book

The core problem is supply and demand: there are far more applicants than daily appointment slots. VFS doesn't publish a release schedule, so new slots appear at unpredictable times — early morning, late at night, during the week or on weekends. There's no pattern you can exploit with manual checking.

On top of this, VFS uses Cloudflare bot protection. Browser extensions that auto-refresh the page will quickly get flagged and locked out, making them counterproductive. The only approach that works reliably is a monitor that behaves like a real browser session.
