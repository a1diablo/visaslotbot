About the Switzerland VFS centre in London

The Embassy of Switzerland's Regional Consular Centre in London delegates visa processing to VFS Global. The booking portal for Switzerland in the UK is at visa.vfsglobal.com/gbr/en/che, with application centres in London, Manchester, Edinburgh, and Cardiff.

Note a recent change: Switzerland now runs its own dedicated VFS centre in the UK. It is no longer bundled with the Austria centre — Switzerland has separate appointments, a separate calendar, and its own booking flow.
