The Netherlands Visa Application Centre in London

The Netherlands processes Schengen visa applications in the UK through VFS Global. The London centre is the Netherlands Visa Application Centre London (NAKN), and the booking portal is at visa.vfsglobal.com/gbr/en/nld.

To book, you select the centre, choose "Schengen Visa" as the category, then "Tourism" as the subcategory. If appointments are available, a calendar appears. Most of the time, it doesn't.

Why slots are almost impossible to find

The Netherlands VAC London handles a large volume of applications with a fixed number of daily slots. VFS doesn't release a schedule for when new appointments become available. They appear at random times — early morning, late night, weekends — and get booked within minutes.

VFS uses Cloudflare protection on the booking system. Browser extensions that auto-refresh the page trigger bot detection quickly, locking you out. Manual checking is exhausting and unreliable. The only effective solution is a monitor that mimics real browser behaviour.

Tips for booking faster

Have your documents ready. When an alert arrives, the only delay should be your internet connection. Have your passport, photos, bank statements, insurance, and accommodation bookings already compiled.

Keep the VFS portal open. Stay on the Netherlands booking page in a browser tab. This saves 15–30 seconds when a slot appears.

Take the first available date. Don't try to find a convenient date — take whatever appears. You can try to reschedule once you have a confirmed appointment.

Enable Telegram push notifications. Slots can appear at 3am. If you see the notification 20 minutes later, the slot is gone.

London vs Dublin: which centre should you use?

If you're in the UK, the London centre (NAKN) is the natural choice. If you're in Ireland, the Dublin centre covers the same Netherlands Schengen visa. The two systems are independent — an appointment at one centre doesn't affect availability at the other.
